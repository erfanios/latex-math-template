\begin{leftbar}
\Huge Geometric Series
\end{leftbar}

A geometric series is an infinite sum of the form
\(\sum_{q=0}^{\infty} p^q = 1 + p + p^2 + p^3 + \cdots\).\
It converges exactly when the absolute value of the common ratio is less than one, that is, \(|p| < 1\). In this case,
\(\sum_{q=0}^{\infty} p^q = \frac{1}{1-p}\).\
If \(|p| \geq 1\), the summands do not approach zero, so the series diverges.
